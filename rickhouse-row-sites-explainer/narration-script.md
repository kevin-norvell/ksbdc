## Presenter notes

Extra detail for each scene, all from the same Help Center facts. Use these if you have more time or get questions.

1. **Open.** The bakery is fictional. It's a training demo, so the site in the video carries a "Fictional business • Training demonstration" footer.
2. **Meet the bakery.** Hours are 6 AM to 2 PM, Monday through Saturday, closed Sunday. The menu: cinnamon rolls, sourdough bread, chocolate chunk cookies with sea salt, and coffee.
3. **The ask.** You can include your content, files, photos, links, and any constraints in that first message.
4. **What it is.** The workflow is: describe it, review the private preview, ask for changes until it's right, then publish.
5. **What you need.** Sites isn't available on Free or Go, or in the ChatGPT Classic app. Usage is included up to plan-specific beta limits, and ChatGPT warns you as you get close.
6. **Build it.** Changes are plain-language requests. There's no special syntax.
7. **Before you publish.** Every deployment URL is a production URL. Saving a version and reviewing it first keeps unfinished changes from going live.
8. **Go live.** A new site starts visible only to its owner. "Anyone on the internet" makes it public. After you publish, use Visit to open the site or Copy link to share it.
9. **Your own domain.** Sites doesn't register domains, so buy the domain first. The full steps: open Site settings, select Add domain, enter the domain, copy the DNS records Sites gives you into your domain provider, wait a few minutes, then refresh the status.
10. **Keep it current.** You can also reopen the chat, or pick the site from Sites in the sidebar, and describe the change. Edit site works from a desktop browser for the site's owner. Typing in "Describe your edits" and selecting "Send edits" starts an edit, and the live site doesn't change until you publish.
11. **Responsibilities.**
    - **Forms:** if your site has contact forms, order requests, or sign-ups, you're responsible for how you collect and use that information and for following privacy laws.
    - **Payments:** use a third-party payment processor. You're responsible for setting it up, plus fulfillment, refunds, customer support, and taxes.
    - **Not allowed:** Protected Health Information, payment card data handled directly by the site, sites aimed at kids under 13, phishing, and impersonating people or businesses.
    - **Taking a site down:** restrict access in Share, or use Delete site. Deletion is permanent.
12. **Close.** If you're presenting live, this is a good place to pause on the end card and take questions.
